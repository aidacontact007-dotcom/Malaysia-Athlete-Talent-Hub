About the System

Built to Record
Every Step an Athlete Takes.

Malaysia Athlete Database is a central registry that connects athletes, coaches, sports schools and national governing bodies in one verified, searchable record.

Why This System Exists

One Official Record, Not Scattered Files.

Athlete records used to be spread across paper forms, association spreadsheets and separate school systems. This system brings it all together — so an athlete's performance and achievements don't get lost between levels.

01

For Athletes

Keep one profile for your entire sporting career — from school level, to state, to national.

02

For Coaches

Check an athlete's record and readiness before team selection or programme placement.

03

For Associations

Access structured data by sport category, state and competition level for talent planning.

System Objectives

What This System Aims to Do.

Beyond record-keeping, the database is built around a set of clear goals for athletes, coaches and associations.

  • Provide every athlete with one official, verified profile for their entire career.
  • Give coaches and associations a reliable way to check athlete status and readiness.
  • Track performance and achievements consistently from school level to international level.
  • To promote the athlete to teams and coaches — helping athletes market their talent to potential recruiters.

How It Works
